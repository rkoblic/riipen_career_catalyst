# Change Log

*How to use this template: This is a running record of the substantive feedback your team has received during the project and how you addressed it. Start it after the Direction Check and keep it current through development. Submit it alongside the Final Deliverables. It's the basis for the Responsiveness to Feedback dimension of the rubric. Add one row per piece of substantive feedback. The rubric is asking whether your team engaged meaningfully with the input it received, not whether every comma change was logged. Reconstructing weeks of changes the night before submission is much harder than maintaining the log as you go. The first three rows below are examples to show what different feedback types look like; replace them with your team's own entries.*
