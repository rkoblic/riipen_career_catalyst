# Presentation Outline

*How to use this template: Use this template to draft the first version of your team's Final Presentation outline. The outline is a working document the team can argue with early, not a finished deck. Refinement and rehearsal come later, and the outline is submitted as a checkpoint at that point. A draft that fills all four sections, even roughly, is the goal for this first sitting. Leaving the outline half-complete (key messages without speaker assignments, or assignments without handoff language) means the team will end up doing both kinds of thinking from scratch later. Finish a full first pass now, then revise through rehearsal.*

## Part 3: Visual approach

*Decide section by section which sections need a slide and which work better without one. Slides aren't required for every section. A short section where the speaker is making a concrete recommendation sometimes works better with nothing on screen, because the audience listens more closely when there's nothing to read. A useful test: any slide that works as an email attachment without a speaker is probably doing too much.*
