# Employer End-of-Project Evaluation — Form build content

For the Riipen Experience Manager building this evaluation in the survey tool. Contains the headers, employer-facing text, scales, and field types.

Audience: the employer contact for the project, completed once per team. The full instrument is seven items and should take about five minutes. Items 1–3 use the same BARS anchors as the mid-project employer survey, with prompts re-framed to cover the full project.

## Survey settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Audience | Employer contact (one response per team) |
| Timing | Week 15, ideally within one week of the Final Presentation |
| Estimated completion time | 5 minutes |
| Grading role | Auto-aggregated into the team's End-of-Project Employer Feedback gradebook item, worth 3.5% of each team member's final course grade — one of five graded items inside Professional Engagement. |
| Editable after submission? | No |
| Visibility | Items 1–3 (dimension scores), Item 4 (Deliverable Quality), and Item 5 (Partnership Quality) are shared with the team per institutional default. Items 6 (open-ended for instructor) and 7 (satisfaction signal) are instructor-only. The platform should allow institutions to override visibility on Items 4 and 5 if a partner university prefers to keep those private. |

## Pre-survey framing

Shown above Item 1 on the form.

Thank you for working with this team across the project, and for taking five more minutes to close the loop with this end-of-project evaluation. This is the summative version of the brief survey you completed at mid-project. It covers the same three rated dimensions framed across the full engagement, adds two summative items on the final deliverables and your overall partnership experience, and ends with two short signals for the instructor.

Two notes on scope. Items 1–3 ask you to consider the entire project from start to finish, not only the recent weeks. Items 4 and 5 are end-of-project judgments that the mid-project survey did not cover.

Items 1–5 may be shared with the team as closure on the partnership. Items 6 and 7 are read only by the instructor.

## Item 1 — Communication

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *Considering the entire project from start to finish, how effectively has the team communicated with you — in meetings, emails, and follow-ups?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | Interactions are consistently clear, professional, and well-organized. Emails are concise and action-oriented. Meetings are productive. I always know where we stand. |
| 3 | Good | Interactions are generally clear and professional. Communication is adequate — I get the information I need, though occasionally I have to ask for clarification. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Communication is inconsistent. Some interactions are professional, others feel disorganized or unclear. I have had to chase the team for updates or clarification more than once. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Communication is poor. Emails go unanswered or are confusing. Meetings feel unprepared. I am frequently unsure what the team is working on or where things stand. |

## Item 2 — Preparedness

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *Considering the entire project, how well-prepared has the team been for your interactions — did they come to meetings with clear agendas, questions, and organized materials?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | The team arrives at every meeting with a clear agenda, organized materials, and thoughtful questions. They make productive use of our time together. I can tell they have prepared. |
| 3 | Good | The team is generally prepared for meetings. They have materials and questions ready, though the organization could sometimes be tighter. Our time together is mostly productive. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Preparation is uneven. Some meetings feel well-organized while others seem improvised. The team sometimes lacks clear questions or materials, making our interactions less productive than they could be. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | The team regularly comes to meetings unprepared. Agendas are missing or vague. Materials are incomplete or disorganized. Our meetings are often unproductive. |

## Item 3 — Responsiveness

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *Considering the entire project, how responsive has the team been to your input — did they follow up on your feedback, ask clarifying questions, and keep you updated between meetings?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | The team consistently follows up on my feedback. They ask clarifying questions when something is unclear. They keep me updated between meetings without being asked. I feel like an active partner in this project. |
| 3 | Good | The team generally follows up on feedback and responds to my input. They keep me reasonably informed between meetings. I occasionally need to prompt them for updates, but overall the communication flow works. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Follow-up on my feedback is inconsistent. I am not always sure whether my input has been heard or acted on. I have had to reach out to check on progress more than I expected. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | The team rarely follows up on feedback or keeps me updated. I feel out of the loop between meetings. My input does not seem to influence the direction of their work. |

## Item 4 — Deliverable Quality

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *Based on the final work product, how would you rate the overall quality of what this team delivered?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | The final deliverables exceed my expectations. The work is thorough, well-organized, and directly useful to my organization. I could share this with colleagues or leadership as-is. |
| 3 | Good | The final deliverables meet my expectations. The work is solid and useful, though there are areas where it could be stronger. With minor adjustments, I could use this in my organization. |
| 2 | Below expectations | The final deliverables partially meet my expectations. Some sections are useful, but others need significant work before I could act on them. The overall quality is uneven. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | The final deliverables do not meet my expectations. The work is incomplete, off-target, or of insufficient quality to be useful to my organization. |

## Item 5 — Partnership Quality

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), four options
Required: Yes

Prompt shown to employer:

Would you work with this team again on a future project?

Options shown to employer:

- Definitely yes
- Probably yes
- Probably not
- Definitely not

## Item 6 — Open-ended feedback for the instructor

Visibility on this item is restricted to the instructor — the team does not see this response. The form should note this above the question.

Framing shown to employer:

This response is read only by the instructor. It is not shared with the team.

Field type: Long text (textarea)
Required: No

Prompt shown to employer:

Is there anything you would like the instructor to know about your experience working with this team? Anything the structured items above did not capture — an exceptional individual contribution, a specific concern, or context that helps interpret the ratings.

## Item 7 — Satisfaction signal (instructor-only)

Visibility on this item is restricted to the instructor — the team does not see this response. The form should note this above the question.

Framing shown to employer:

This final rating is read only by the instructor. It helps the instructor check the structured ratings above against an overall impression.

Field type: Numeric scale, 1–10 (radio buttons or slider)
Required: Yes

Prompt shown to employer:

Overall, how satisfied are you with this team's professionalism throughout the project?

## Submit confirmation

After submission, show a brief acknowledgement:

Thank you for completing this evaluation and for partnering with this team across the project. Your structured ratings and your "would you work with this team again" response will be shared with the team as closure on the partnership. Your final two responses go only to the instructor.
