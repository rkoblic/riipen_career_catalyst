# Weekly Planning Worksheet

*How to use this template: Fill this out at the start of each week, before the week's deliverables and meetings begin to pile up. The goal is a realistic plan you'll actually follow, not a perfect one. Revisit it midweek to adjust, and return to the bottom section at the end of the week for a short check-in. Save a copy per week so you can look back at what you planned versus what happened.*
