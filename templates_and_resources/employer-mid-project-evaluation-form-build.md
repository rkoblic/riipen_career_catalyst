# Employer Mid-Project Evaluation — Form build content

For the Riipen Experience Manager building this evaluation in the survey tool. Contains the headers, employer-facing text, scales, and field types.

Audience: the employer contact for the project, completed once per team. The full instrument is five items and should take about three minutes.

## Survey settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Audience | Employer contact (one response per team) |
| Timing | Week 8, ideally within 48 hours of Progress Review #1 (Week 7) |
| Estimated completion time | 3 minutes |
| Grading role | Auto-aggregated into the team's Mid-Project Employer Feedback gradebook item, worth 1.5% of each team member's final course grade — one of five graded items inside Professional Engagement. |
| Editable after submission? | No |
| Visibility | Items 1–3 (dimension scores) and Item 4 (open-ended response) are shared with the team. Item 5 (confidence signal) is instructor-only. |

## Pre-survey framing

Shown above Item 1 on the form.

Thanks for taking a few minutes to give this team feedback at the mid-point of the project. This survey is brief — three rated items, one open-ended question, and a short signal for the instructor — and should take about three minutes.

Two notes on what we are and are not asking. The rated items are scoped to what you can directly observe: how the team has communicated, prepared for your interactions, and responded to your input. Internal team dynamics and the quality of any final deliverable are assessed separately and are not part of this survey.

Your dimension scores and your open-ended response go back to the team in time for them to act on the feedback in the second half of the project. The final question is read only by the instructor.

## Item 1 — Communication

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *How effectively has the team communicated with you — in meetings, emails, and follow-ups?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | Interactions are consistently clear, professional, and well-organized. Emails are concise and action-oriented. Meetings are productive. I always know where we stand. |
| 3 | Good | Interactions are generally clear and professional. Communication is adequate — I get the information I need, though occasionally I have to ask for clarification. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Communication is inconsistent. Some interactions are professional, others feel disorganized or unclear. I have had to chase the team for updates or clarification more than once. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Communication is poor. Emails go unanswered or are confusing. Meetings feel unprepared. I am frequently unsure what the team is working on or where things stand. |

## Item 2 — Preparedness

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *How well-prepared has the team been for your interactions — did they come to meetings with clear agendas, questions, and organized materials?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | The team arrives at every meeting with a clear agenda, organized materials, and thoughtful questions. They make productive use of our time together. I can tell they have prepared. |
| 3 | Good | The team is generally prepared for meetings. They have materials and questions ready, though the organization could sometimes be tighter. Our time together is mostly productive. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Preparation is uneven. Some meetings feel well-organized while others seem improvised. The team sometimes lacks clear questions or materials, making our interactions less productive than they could be. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | The team regularly comes to meetings unprepared. Agendas are missing or vague. Materials are incomplete or disorganized. Our meetings are often unproductive. |

## Item 3 — Responsiveness

Sub-heading question shown to employer: *How responsive has the team been to your input — did they follow up on your feedback, ask clarifying questions, and keep you updated between meetings?*

Field type: Radio buttons (single select), 1–4
Required: Yes

Options shown to employer:

| Rating | Descriptor | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Excellent | The team consistently follows up on my feedback. They ask clarifying questions when something is unclear. They keep me updated between meetings without being asked. I feel like an active partner in this project. |
| 3 | Good | The team generally follows up on feedback and responds to my input. They keep me reasonably informed between meetings. I occasionally need to prompt them for updates, but overall the communication flow works. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | Follow-up on my feedback is inconsistent. I am not always sure whether my input has been heard or acted on. I have had to reach out to check on progress more than I expected. |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | The team rarely follows up on feedback or keeps me updated. I feel out of the loop between meetings. My input does not seem to influence the direction of their work. |

## Item 4 — Open-ended feedback for the team

Field type: Long text (textarea)
Required: Yes (allow short responses, e.g., a single sentence)

Prompt shown to employer:

Is there anything specific you would like the team to do differently in how they communicate or work with you for the remainder of the project?

Help text shown below the field:

Your response will be shared with the team verbatim. If you have no suggestions, "No changes needed — keep doing what you're doing" is a fine answer and is itself useful feedback.

## Item 5 — Confidence signal (instructor-only)

Visibility on this item is restricted to the instructor — the team does not see this response. The form should note this above the question so the employer can answer candidly.

Framing shown to employer:

This last question is read only by the instructor. It is not shared with the team and is not used to grade the team. It exists so the instructor can check in with you or the team early if something is at risk.

Field type: Numeric scale, 1–10 (radio buttons or slider)
Required: Yes

Prompt shown to employer:

At this point in the project, how confident are you that this team will deliver quality work?

## Submit confirmation

After submission, show a brief acknowledgement:

Thank you. Your dimension scores and your open-ended response will be shared with the team this week. Your confidence rating goes only to the instructor.
