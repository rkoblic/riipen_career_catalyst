# Week 11 — Quality and Craft
## Content Development Plan

---

## Content Architecture

Week 11 opens the Refinement phase and is a **Pattern A (meeting-centered)** week with a required checkpoint submission. The Near-Final Review is the week's anchor event — a document-sharing meeting where the employer reviews the assembled deliverable package and the team brings targeted questions about quality and completeness. Three short modules (11.02, 11.03, 11.04) feed the anchor directly; all other activities are project work with no instructional content needed. The Near-Final Package submission gets its own page per the rule that required checkpoints are findable. The week reduces to five content pieces: Week Overview → Quality and Craft module → Near-Final Package checkpoint → Near-Final Review → What's Next.

---

## Content Production Plan

### 1. Week 11 Overview
**Type:** Orientation text + AI-generated video
**Absorbs:** 11.01

Orients learners to the Refinement phase shift: the work is largely built; this week is about making it ready for a professional audience. Names the Near-Final Review and Near-Final Package as the week's two anchor items. Flags that this is a document-sharing meeting, not a formal presentation — the document should be able to stand on its own. Covers what's due and suggested order of completion.

**Sub-elements:**
- Week framing: the shift from building to polishing, and what that demands professionally (AI-generated video, ~1–2 min)
- What's due this week: Near-Final Package (team checkpoint, completion-based) (AI-generated text, ~75 words)
- Suggested order of completion (AI-generated text, ~100 words)

**Time check:** 5 min allocated → ~175 words text + short video fills this appropriately. ✓

---

### 2. Quality and Craft
**Type:** Standalone module
**Absorbs:** 11.02, 11.03

Two closely related modules covering the quality standard for this phase and the self-review protocol learners apply before submitting the Near-Final Package and before the employer sees the work. Kept as one page because both modules serve the same moment (internal review gate) and the combined content is appropriately sized for a 30-minute module.

**Sub-elements:**

**What "quality" means at this stage**
- The distinction between complete and polished in professional work; what employers notice when they review a document independently (formatting consistency, error-free prose, visual coherence, logical flow) (AI-generated text, ~300 words)
- Connection to the Deliverable Quality dimension of the Final Deliverables rubric (AI-generated text, ~100 words)
- Curated link: resource on professional document quality and editing standards (Curated link + framing, ~6–8 min)

**Self-review before client review**
- How to read your own work as a fresh reader; the errors that hide in plain sight — inconsistent terminology, orphaned references, formatting drift across sections (AI-generated video, ~1–2 min — primary teaching vehicle; page text becomes scannable checklist)
- Self-review checklist: terminology consistency, alignment with project brief, alignment with employer feedback received, formatting and visual coherence, actionability of recommendations, prose clarity (Checklist, ~6 items)
- How to use AI to supplement the self-review: extending the Week 10 AI as Quality Reviewer strategy — "identify the three weakest sections and explain why" (AI-generated text, ~150 words)

**Time check:** 11.02 (15 min) + 11.03 (15 min) = 30 min allocated → ~550 words text (~3 min reading) + video (~2 min) + curated link (~6–8 min) + checklist (~3 min to work through) + thinking time ≈ 30 min. ✓

### 3. Near-Final Package
**Type:** Assignment prompt with embedded guidance
**Absorbs:** 11.11

Required team checkpoint with its own findable page. Follows the same structure as the WIP Summary and Draft Deliverable pages — "What this checkpoint is (and isn't)" framing up front, completion criteria clearly stated, AI guidance and disclosure requirement explicit.

**Sub-elements:**
- What this checkpoint is (and isn't): near-final work ready for employer scrutiny; not a first draft relabeled; not quality-graded but substantive completion standard applies (AI-generated text, ~150 words)
- What the package should include: all deliverable components assembled, Change Log attached, formatting consistent throughout (AI-generated text, ~200 words)
- Completion criteria: specific, stated plainly — what meets the standard and what doesn't (AI-generated text, ~100 words)
- AI guidance: polishing and editing encouraged; quality review application appropriate; AI cannot substitute for the team's own research and recommendations; defend-your-work standard applies at Near-Final Review and Final Presentation (AI-generated text, ~150 words)
- AI disclosure requirement and submission instructions (AI-generated text, ~75 words)

**Time check:** 11.11 (20 min) → ~675 words text (~3–4 min reading) + time to finalize and submit (~15 min). ✓

### 4. Near-Final Review
**Type:** Assignment prompt with embedded guidance
**Absorbs:** 11.04, 11.07, 11.08, 11.09, 11.10

Single combined page covering preparation, the meeting itself, and the debrief — following Rachel's pattern for working session meetings (one page, not three). This meeting is explicitly a document-sharing session, not a formal presentation; the page should make that distinction clear and frame preparation accordingly. Module 11.04 (Preparing for the Near-Final Review) is the instructional core; the internal review gate (11.07) and prep session (11.08) are embedded as recommended steps before the meeting.

**Sub-elements:**

**What makes this meeting different**
- How the Near-Final Review differs from Check-In #1 and Check-In #2: the document speaks for itself; the employer reviews it independently or during the meeting; the team's job is to walk through the Change Log and ask targeted questions, not to present (AI-generated text, ~200 words)

**Preparing**
- Running the internal review gate before the employer sees the work: is the package clean, navigable, and self-explanatory? (AI-generated text, ~150 words — embedded recommendation, not a tracked activity)
- Preparing the Change Log walkthrough: how to present evidence of responsiveness to prior feedback; what to include (feedback received, decision made, where it shows up in the document) (AI-generated text, ~200 words)
- Developing targeted questions: what kind of feedback is useful at this stage vs. too early-stage to raise; examples of near-final-quality questions (AI-generated text, ~200 words)
- Using AI to generate likely employer questions: continuation of stress-testing approach from earlier check-ins (AI-generated text, ~100 words)

**During the meeting**
- Suggested time breakdown for 30 minutes: Change Log walkthrough (~5 min) → targeted questions (~18–20 min) → employer observations (~5 min) (AI-generated text, ~150 words)
- What to capture in notes; how to handle ambiguous feedback in the moment (AI-generated text, ~100 words)

**After the meeting**
- Debrief immediately; update the Change Log; translate feedback into the final revision priority list before the team disperses (AI-generated text, ~150 words)

**Time check:** 11.04 (15 min instruction) + 11.07 (30 min gate) + 11.08 (30 min prep) + 11.09 (60 min hold / ~30 min actual) + 11.10 (30 min debrief) = 155 min total activity → ~1,050 words instructional text (~5 min reading) fills the module time (15 min); project work and meeting time (remaining ~140 min) are the activities themselves. ✓

### 5. What's Next
**Type:** Transitional prompt
**Absorbs:** nothing (forward-looking)

Brief bridging page to Weeks 12–13. The Final Check-In (optional) and Final Deliverables submission are the remaining major milestones. Near-Final Review feedback becomes the input for the final production push. Frames what "final" means in this context and what the team should prioritize based on what they heard.
