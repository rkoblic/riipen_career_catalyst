# The pieces start to come together

This week the sections your team drafted independently start to come together as a document. At the same time, your peer evaluation results and the employer's mid-project survey both land, and there's another check-in with your employer on the calendar.

It's a busy week, and it's designed that way. Your team's first move, before the assembly work or the check-in prep, is to process the feedback that arrives this week. Peer evaluation results are yours to work through on your own; the employer's survey is processed in a structured team session, where each person also brings one or two commitments they've made themselves. Doing the personal work first is what makes the team conversation worth having.

## What's due this week

**Draft Deliverable.** Team submission, completion-based checkpoint. Your assembled draft, submitted to the platform for completion verification. This is a working draft that reflects your team's current best effort, not a polished final product. The deliverable is more for you than it is for the employer. Putting the pieces together is what helps your team see what's working and what still needs to come together before the near-final version goes out.

**Reflection #3.** Individual submission, graded (rubric-based, contributes to the Reflection Portfolio component of your final grade). Draws from the most intensive feedback and development period of the course. Due this week.

---

## Suggested order of completion

1. **Read the Team Processing Session page first.** Work through your peer evaluation results individually and land on one or two commitments to bring to the team. Then hold your team's session, where you'll share commitments and process the employer's mid-project survey together.
2. **Read the Draft Deliverable page and work through the integration, actionability, and scope skills with your team.** Run the internal review gate on your assembled draft. Submit the draft before your next check-in with the employer.
3. **Read the check-in page and run your prep session.** The question framing has shifted since your last check-in, and the quality guidance on that page is worth reading before you prepare your questions.
4. **Complete the check-in** with your employer.
5. **Read the Reflection #3 module, then draft and submit your reflection individually.** The team processing agreement you produced earlier in the week is one good anchor for the reflection.
