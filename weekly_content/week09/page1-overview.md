# The first complete picture

This week the work becomes visible as a whole for the first time. Sections drafted independently now have to hold together as a document. Feedback from multiple directions arrives at once: peer evaluation results, the employer mid-project survey, and Check-In #2 with your employer. And you have two deliverables to submit.

It's the most concentrated week of the Development phase, and it's designed that way. The skills you're practicing here, assembling and strengthening work under pressure, are the ones that distinguish teams that produce something genuinely useful from teams that hand in something that was finished in the technical sense.

[VIDEO: What assembling a draft actually tests]

---

## What's due this week

**Draft Deliverable** — team submission, completion-based checkpoint. Your assembled draft, with the team processing agreement appended. This is not a polished final product — it is a genuine working draft that reflects your current best effort. Due before Check-In #2 so the employer is responding to real work.

**Reflection #3** — individual submission, graded (rubric-based, contributes approximately 5% of your final grade as part of the Reflection Portfolio). Draws from the most intensive feedback and production period of the course. Due this week.

---

## Suggested order of completion

1. Read the Strengthening Your Draft module. Apply the multi-author integration and actionability guidance before your team runs the internal review gate. Don't skip the review gate — submitting a draft none of you has read end-to-end is how teams get caught off guard in Check-In #2.
2. Read the Peer Evaluation Conversation module, then hold your Team Processing Session. Your peer evaluation results and employer mid-project survey results are available — address them before the week runs out.
3. Review the Draft Deliverable checkpoint page and submit the draft with the team processing agreement appended. This is due before Check-In #2.
4. Read the Check-In #2 page and run your prep session. The question framing has shifted since Check-In #1 — the quality guidance on that page is worth reading before you prepare your questions.
5. Complete Check-In #2.
6. Read the Reflection #3 module, then draft and submit your reflection individually.
