# The first complete picture

This week the work becomes visible as a whole for the first time. Sections drafted independently now have to hold together as a document. Feedback from multiple directions arrives at once: peer evaluation results, the employer mid-project survey, and another check-in with your employer. And you have two deliverables to submit.

It's the most concentrated stretch of the course so far, and it's designed that way. Your team's first move, before the assembly work or the check-in prep, is a single session to process the peer evaluation results and the employer's survey together. That conversation is the one most workplaces don't run until something has already gone wrong.

---

## What's due this week

**Draft Deliverable.** Team submission, completion-based checkpoint. Your assembled draft, submitted to the platform for completion verification. This is a working draft that reflects your team's current best effort, not a polished final product. The deliverable is more for you than it is for the employer. Putting the pieces together is what helps your team find integration problems before the near-final version goes out.

**Reflection #3.** Individual submission, graded (rubric-based, contributes to the Reflection Portfolio component of your final grade). Draws from the most intensive feedback and production period of the course. Due this week.

---

## Suggested order of completion

1. Read the Team Processing Session page, then hold your team's session. Your peer evaluation results and employer mid-project survey results are available; address them before the week runs out.
2. Read the Draft Deliverable page and work through the integration, actionability, and scope skills with your team. Run the internal review gate on your assembled draft. Submit the draft before your next check-in with the employer.
3. Read the check-in page and run your prep session. The question framing has shifted since your last check-in, and the quality guidance on that page is worth reading before you prepare your questions.
4. Complete the check-in with your employer.
5. Read the Reflection #3 module, then draft and submit your reflection individually. The team processing agreement you produced earlier in the week is one good anchor for the reflection.
