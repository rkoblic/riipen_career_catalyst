# Near-Final Package

The Near-Final Package is the required team checkpoint that opens this week's meeting with your employer. Submit it through the Riipen platform before you meet. When you submit, the package is automatically shared with your employer, so aim to submit well in advance so they have a chance to review it.

---

## What this checkpoint is (and isn't)

This is near-final work. The deliverable is assembled, internally consistent, and polished to something the team is willing to stand behind in a meeting. It is not a first draft with a new label.

The Near-Final Package is completion-based, which means it isn't quality-graded directly. But a substantive completion standard applies. Work that's clearly incomplete, missing major components, or hasn't been through any quality review doesn't meet the bar.

The quality of the conversation at the meeting depends heavily on the quality of what you submit. A near-final package earns specific, useful feedback when the employer does look at sections closely. A package that isn't there yet tends to get general observations that are harder to act on, no matter how much time the employer spent with it.

---

## What the package includes

The Near-Final Package has two components: the assembled deliverable and the Change Log.

**The assembled deliverable.** Every section in one place, in the correct order, with consistent formatting throughout. If your deliverable includes multiple documents or a primary document with supporting materials, submit everything as a single package rather than separate files.

**The Change Log.** Your team's running record of feedback received and how you've addressed it, current through the most recent employer check-in. The employer reads it as part of evaluating how the team engaged with prior feedback.

The package meets the completion standard when:

- All deliverable components are present and assembled in a single submission
- The Change Log is attached and current through the most recent employer check-in
- The package has been through a team self-review
- Formatting is consistent throughout the document

Work that's missing major components, has a Change Log that stops several weeks before submission, or hasn't been through any quality review doesn't meet the standard.

---

## AI and submission

Polishing and editing are appropriate and encouraged at this stage. Using the AI quality reviewer approach to supplement your self-review pass is exactly the right application of that skill.

What AI cannot do here: generate the analysis, findings, or recommendations that make up your deliverable, or write qualitative sections your team hasn't drafted. The defend-your-work standard applies because the team is walking the employer through the package. Anything on the page should be something someone on the team can explain in the team's own voice. If AI generated something your team can't explain, it doesn't belong in the package.

**AI disclosure** is required on this submission. Include a brief statement at the end of your package describing how AI was used during the production and polishing process. If AI was not used, a one-sentence statement noting that is sufficient.

Submit the complete Near-Final Package through the Riipen platform. Include:
- The assembled deliverable (all components)
- The Change Log
- Your AI disclosure statement
