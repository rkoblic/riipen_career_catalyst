# Near-Final Package

The Near-Final Package is the required team checkpoint that opens this week's document review meeting with your employer. Submit it through the Riipen platform before the Near-Final Review meeting. The employer reads it in advance.

---

## What this checkpoint is (and isn't)

This is near-final work, ready for your employer to read independently. That means the deliverable is assembled, internally consistent, and polished to the point where it can stand on its own without the team in the room to explain it. It is not a first draft with a new label.

The Near-Final Package is completion-based, which means it isn't quality-graded directly. But a substantive completion standard applies. Work that's clearly incomplete, missing major components, or hasn't been through any quality review doesn't meet the bar. The employer reads this package before the meeting and forms their own assessment. What they bring into the Near-Final Review is their read, not a reaction to what you tell them.

The Near-Final Review conversation is substantially different when the package is genuinely near-final. The employer can give you meaningful, specific feedback. When the package isn't there yet, the conversation tends toward general observations that are harder to act on.

---

## What the package should include

**All deliverable components, assembled.** Every section of your deliverable should be in one place, in the correct order, with consistent formatting throughout. If your deliverable includes multiple documents or a primary document with supporting materials, submit everything as a single package rather than separate files.

**The Change Log, attached.** Your Change Log is the running record of feedback your team has received and how you've addressed it. It should be current through your most recent employer check-in. Attach it alongside the deliverable. The employer reads the Change Log as part of evaluating how the team engaged with prior feedback.

---

## Completion criteria

The Near-Final Package meets the completion standard when:

- All deliverable components are present and assembled in a single submission
- The Change Log is attached and current through the most recent employer check-in
- The package has been through a team self-review and is ready for the employer to read independently
- Formatting is consistent throughout the document

Work that's missing major components, has a Change Log that stops several weeks before submission, or hasn't been through any quality review doesn't meet the standard.

---

## AI guidance

Polishing and editing are appropriate and encouraged at this stage. Using the AI quality reviewer approach to supplement your self-review pass is exactly the right application of that skill.

What AI cannot do here: generate the analysis, findings, or recommendations that make up your deliverable, or write qualitative sections your team hasn't drafted. The Near-Final Review uses a defend-your-work standard. The employer may walk through specific sections and ask your team to explain the reasoning behind a recommendation or the evidence behind a finding. If AI generated something your team can't explain, it doesn't belong in the package.

AI-assisted editing and quality review are appropriate uses. AI-generated content substituting for your team's research and analysis is not.

---

## AI disclosure and submission

AI disclosure is required on this submission. Include a brief statement at the end of your package describing how AI was used during the production and polishing process. If AI was not used, a one-sentence statement noting that is sufficient.

Submit the complete Near-Final Package through the Riipen platform. Include:
- The assembled deliverable (all components)
- The Change Log
- Your AI disclosure statement
