# Video Script: Your Team Starts Now

This week, you meet your team. By the end of it, you'll have a working agreement with them and you'll have sent your first professional message to your employer contact.

The topics you cover with your peers may not feel immediately important. How will we communicate? Who does what? What happens when we disagree? But working through those questions now is what great teams do to avoid the much harder conversations later, when the project is already in motion.

The message to the employer is also an important first impression. It's your team's introduction to a working professional who will judge your collaboration partly by how that message reads.

So get your team into a meeting, write the charter together, and send the outreach message. These are all foundations for your team's success.
