# Video Script: The skill gap

Picture a student preparing for her team's kickoff with a small solar installation company in the Midwest. She opens ChatGPT, types "Tell me about the renewable energy industry," and gets back a wall of generic text. She copies the parts that look useful and shows up to the meeting feeling prepared. Then the client asks a specific question about a recent state policy change, and she has nothing.

Before her next employer call, she briefs the same tool differently: "I'm preparing for a meeting with a small solar installation company in the Midwest. What should I know about their competitive landscape, recent regulatory changes, and the biggest challenges companies like this are facing? Flag anything I should verify against current sources." She gets a focused, structured response. She spends twenty minutes verifying the claims that matter most. At the next meeting, she asks an informed question that opens up a part of the project her team hadn't seen.

What changed was how she briefed the tool: enough context to make the answer specific, a clear ask, and a built-in instruction to flag what she'd need to check. The three principles on this page are the longer version of that.
