# From draft to deliverable

This is a development week. Most of your time will go to drafting and building your assigned piece of the team's deliverable. The rest of this page is here to support that work.

The four skill modules below cover situations that come up during development: turning a plan into a finished piece, giving feedback on a teammate's work, taking in feedback on yours, and handling data and visuals. Skim each and dig into what fits your situation now. Others may matter later in the week, or for a teammate whose piece looks different. Your project will also raise questions these modules don't answer, and finding the right resource is part of the work.

There's also a checkpoint with your employer this week: Development Check-In #1, the first progress meeting in the Development phase. Your team brings questions; the employer helps you move the work forward. The Work-in-Progress Summary and Development Check-In #1 pages cover what to submit and how to prepare.

## What's due this week

The **Work-in-Progress Summary** is a completion-based team checkpoint, submitted before your Development Check-In #1 meeting. It's a short shared document your team uses to get aligned before talking to the employer, not a polished artifact the employer reads. The Work-in-Progress Summary page walks through what goes in it and what the completion criteria require.

---

## Suggested order of completion

Most of this week is project work. The sequence below is a guide for fitting the supporting content around that work, not a queue to clear before you start drafting.

1. **Start drafting.** You already know what you're building from last week's setup. Get into the work early so the modules below have something concrete to apply to.
2. **Work through the skill modules as they become relevant.** You don't have to read them in order, and you don't have to read them all at once. Pull each one up when you hit the situation it covers.
3. **Read the Work-in-Progress Summary page** and draft the summary as a team. This is both your checkpoint deliverable and your shared starting point for meeting prep.
4. **Read the Development Check-In #1 page** to understand how the meeting runs, then run your team prep session to develop questions and assign roles.
5. **Meet with your employer** for Development Check-In #1, then debrief as a team immediately after while the conversation is fresh.
6. **Keep drafting**, folding in any direction from the meeting where it helps.
