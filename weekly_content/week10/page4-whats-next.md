# What's next

Your team has spent this week pulling independent pieces into one whole. That's the hardest invisible work on any project, and most teams finish this week further along than they realize.

The shape of your deliverable is largely there. Next week, the work changes.

---

## Entering Refinement

Refinement is where work goes from complete to strong. The focus moves from building new sections to making what's there stronger: tighter prose and cleaner logic between the pieces. Your employer's posture shifts too. They stop asking you questions and start reading the work the way a client would.

The first checkpoint of the phase is a near-final version of your deliverable. Your team submits it, then meets with your employer after they've read through it. This is different from the working-session check-ins you've been having. The employer comes in with their own assessment of what's working and what still needs strengthening, and the work itself drives the conversation. The submission is completion-based, not graded against the final rubric, but a more coherent submission gets more specific feedback in return.

---

## How to prepare

- **Carry the internal review gate output into next week.** The list of what still needs to change is your starting point.
- **Audit your Change Log before the near-final submission.** The log has been running since the Direction Check. By now it should be a substantive record of employer feedback received and how your team addressed it. If entries are thin or inconsistent, use the remaining time to catch up. The log is what lets your team show the employer how earlier feedback shaped the near-final version.
- **Reread the Project Deliverables rubric.** The near-final version isn't assessed against it, but the final version is. [The rubric](URL_TBD) is the clearest picture your team has of what good looks like at the end. Reading it now sharpens your sense of where the work needs to end up.
- **Track your AI use this week.** AI disclosure is required on the near-final submission. Tracking as you go keeps the disclosure honest rather than something to reconstruct later.
