# Video script: Presenting for feedback, not approval

The employer has already read your Context Analysis and Project Plan. They have the document. So the meeting isn't a walkthrough of information they already have. The point is to discuss your thinking with them.

That changes what you should prepare for. You're not building a presentation. You're preparing for a conversation where the employer reacts to your approach, asks why you made the choices you made, and tells you what they see differently. That exchange is what makes this meeting worth holding.

Teams that waste this meeting spend 25 of their 30 minutes summarizing a document the employer already read. The better move is to spend most of the time on the parts where you want the employer's input: the areas you're uncertain about, the assumptions you're making, and the tradeoffs you chose.

Your job going in is to know your document well enough to talk about it without reading from it, and to have specific questions ready for the parts where the employer's perspective matters most.
