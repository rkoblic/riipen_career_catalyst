# What's next

You've just had your first real feedback conversation with the employer, and you have a clearer view of where the project is going. That's the foundation for the next phase.

---

## The Development phase

The Discovery phase is coming to a close. The next stretch of the course is the Development phase: building the deliverables you've been scoping and planning. Your approach has been tested against the employer's perspective, and you have a confirmed direction (or a deliberate revision) to work from. That's the foundation you need before the team commits significant time to producing the deliverable.

Development is a different kind of work than what you've done so far. Discovery focused on understanding the problem; Development is where the team turns that understanding into a recommended solution. The pace picks up and the work gets more concrete, and the planning you've done so far is what makes the next stretch manageable.

---

## How to prepare

- **Finish the Direction Check Summary this week.** It's the handoff between Discovery and Development. Capture what the employer surfaced so the team has a shared record before next week's work begins.
- **Revise the project plan based on what you heard.** A scope change, a reordered priority, or a confirmed direction should make it into the plan now, not after drafting starts.
- **Confirm how the team will divide and assemble the deliverable.** Section ownership, the shared-document setup, and a working draft cadence are easier to agree on now than mid-production.
- **Reflection #2 opens the upcoming week.** It asks you to look back on the Discovery phase before turning the page to Development. Be ready to draw on the full arc — research, kickoff, Direction Check.
