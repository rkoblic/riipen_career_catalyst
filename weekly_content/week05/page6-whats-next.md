# What's next

You've just had your first real feedback conversation with the employer. You know what resonated, what shifted, and where the project is heading. That clarity is the setup for everything that comes next.

---

## The Development phase

The Discovery phase is coming to a close. The next stretch of the course is the Development phase: building the deliverables you've been scoping and planning. Your approach has been tested against the employer's perspective, and you have a confirmed direction (or a deliberate revision) to work from. That's exactly the foundation you need before committing significant time to drafting, designing, and assembling.

Development is a different kind of work than what you've done so far. Discovery was about understanding the problem. Development is about coming up with a recommended solution. The pace picks up, the work becomes more concrete, and the quality of your planning directly determines how smoothly things go.

---

## How to prepare

- **Finish the Direction Check Summary this week.** It's the bridge artifact between Discovery and Development. Capture what the employer surfaced so the team has a shared record before next week's work begins.
- **Revise the project plan based on what you heard.** A scope change, a reordered priority, or a confirmed direction should make it into the plan now, not after drafting starts.
- **Confirm how the team will divide and assemble the deliverable.** Section ownership, the shared-document setup, and a working draft cadence are easier to agree on now than mid-production.
- **Reflection #2 opens the upcoming week.** It asks you to look back on the Discovery phase before turning the page to Development. Be ready to draw on the full arc — research, kickoff, Direction Check.
