# What's next

You've completed the mid-project peer evaluation and continued forward production. Two things land next week: another employer check-in and a chance for your team to process the peer evaluation results and the employer's mid-project survey feedback.

---

## Check-In #2

The second employer progress meeting follows the same structure as Check-In #1: your team brings specific questions and decision points, and the employer helps you move forward. The difference is that you're further into the work. The questions you can ask are more specific, and the employer's input can be more targeted.

Start identifying your questions before the week ends. The sections you've been revising this week are a natural source. Anywhere your team made a judgment call about how to address the employer's feedback, or anywhere a revision raised a new question, is a candidate for Check-In #2.

---

## How to prepare

- **Keep advancing your draft.** The more complete your sections are before Check-In #2, the more specific your questions can be.
- **Review your debrief notes from Check-In #1.** Some items your team noted at the time may look different after a week of production. Notes-category feedback can become act-on once you're further in.
- **Skim the Check-In #2 materials** before the week starts so you know what preparation looks like.
- **Hold onto your peer evaluation notes.** When the results come back, you'll want the evidence that informed your own ratings within reach to make sense of what you receive.
