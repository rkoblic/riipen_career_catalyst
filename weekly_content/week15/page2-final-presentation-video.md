# Video Script: Ready, not polished

If you are watching this the morning of your Final Presentation, here is the most useful thing to know. The work that matters most has already happened. The research, the analysis, the rehearsal, the slide review, the feedback your team gave each other last week. That is what you are about to draw on. The next hour is about delivery, not rework.

Ready does not mean polished. Polished is what a thousand rehearsals look like. Ready is what one good rehearsal plus a clear head looks like, and it is the state that actually works for professional presentations.

So here is what the next hour is for. It is for a short walk, or some space away from the material. It is for reading your opening lines once, because a clean start carries the rest. It is for a glance at your handoff cues, a check on your water, and a breath before the session begins.

It is not for rebuilding slides. It is not for rewriting your section. It is not for a fifth coffee.

Nerves are normal, and they tend to fade about thirty seconds after the first speaker starts. You are not going to feel ready before you begin. You are going to feel ready somewhere in the middle, once the version of you that rehearsed this takes over from the version of you that was worrying about it.

The preparation is real. Trust it.
