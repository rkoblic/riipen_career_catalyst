# What's next

The Refinement phase closes with this week's submission, bringing you to the final phase of the course: Integration. In this phase, you shift from finishing the work to telling its story.

---

## The Final Presentation

The Integration phase centers on the Final Presentation: a live, share-your-work meeting with the employer where they can ask questions about any part of the work your team produced. It is the only formal presentation in the closing phase and the last scheduled employer interaction of the course.

The coming week is preparation. The outline your team drafted earlier gets refined and rehearsed, the deck gets built, and the Presentation Outline is submitted as a checkpoint. The Final Presentation itself happens in the final week.

Two other graded pieces of work open in the coming week and close the following week: Reflection #5 and the End-of-Project Peer Evaluation. Both get full previews when they open; there's nothing to prepare for either before then.

---

## How to prepare

- **Submit the Project Deliverables this week.** The presentation work in the coming weeks assumes the deliverable is done.
- **Keep the Change Log current** through this week's revisions and the final check-in if held. The Change Log is referenced in the Final Presentation's defend-your-work moment.
- **Confirm presentation logistics with the employer** (format, date, time, and attendees) if you haven't already. The final check-in page covers the logistics communication.
- **Have your presentation outline ready to pick up** from the drafting you did earlier in the course. Refinement and rehearsal begin in the coming week.
- **Reflection #5 opens next week.** It's the final reflection — looking across the full course arc to claim the single most important thing you learned about how you work, not about the project's subject matter. Drafting begins next week and finalizes the week after, because the analytical move benefits from days of sitting with a candidate anchor.
- **Take a beat before the next phase starts.** Submission is a real accomplishment. Rest, appreciate what your team built, and come back ready for the presentation work in the coming week.
