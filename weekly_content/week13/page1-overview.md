# Finishing well

This is the submission week. The Final Deliverables are due at the end of this week. The module content is deliberately light because the remaining work is execution, not learning. What the week asks for is focused production time and a clear path to submission.

An optional final check-in with the employer is available this week — one last conversation before the Final Presentation in Week 15. Hold it if your team has substantive remaining questions. It is not a default meeting teams are expected to hold.

## What's due this week

**Final Deliverables** (team submission, graded)

This is the project deliverable as specified in your project brief, plus your Change Log and AI disclosure statement. Submit through the Riipen platform before the end of the week.

---

## Suggested order of completion

1. **Hold the team sync first.** Use it to review submission readiness, make the hold/cancel decision on the final check-in, and assign any remaining revision tasks before the production block starts. Teams that skip this sync tend to spend part of the production block on coordination instead of work.
2. **Read the Final Deliverables Submission page.** The Home Stretch mindset frame, the pre-submission checklist, and the Change Log and AI guidance are all on one page. Read it before the production block, not during.
3. **Do the final revisions and assembly.** Apply the checklist from page 2. The triage framework from last week still applies to any last-minute feedback or gaps: requires action, acknowledge in the Change Log, or out of scope.
4. **Run the internal review gate.** A final pass on the assembled package before submission. Use the checklist to run it efficiently.
5. **Submit the Final Deliverables.** When the package passes the checklist, submit. Confirm as a team that the submitted version is the version everyone agreed is final.
6. **Read the final check-in page** if your team is holding the optional meeting with the employer, and complete the presentation logistics communication with the employer regardless.
7. **Read What's Next** for forward prep on Reflection #5 and the End-of-Project Peer Evaluation.
