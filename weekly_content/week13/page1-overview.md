# Finishing well

This is the submission week. The Project Deliverables are due at the end of this week. The module content is deliberately light because the remaining work is execution, not learning. What the week asks for is focused refinement time and a clear path to submission.

Your next biweekly meeting with the employer also falls this week. It's the last scheduled meeting before the Final Presentation in Week 15. Use it if your team has final questions that need answering and a meeting would be helpful.

## What's due this week

**Project Deliverables** (team submission, graded)

This is the project deliverable as specified in your project brief, plus your Change Log and AI disclosure statement. Submit through the Riipen platform before the end of the week.

---

## Suggested order of completion

1. **Hold the team sync first.** Use it to review submission readiness, decide whether to hold or cancel the biweekly meeting, and assign remaining revision tasks before the production block. Teams that skip this sync spend part of the production block on coordination instead of work.
2. **Read the Project Deliverables Submission page.** The Home Stretch frame, pre-submission checklist, and Change Log and AI guidance all live on one page. Read it before the production block starts.
3. **Do the final revisions and assembly.** Apply the checklist from page 2. The triage framework from last week still applies: requires action, acknowledge in the Change Log, or out of scope.
4. **Run the internal review gate.** Use the checklist to make a final pass on the assembled package before submission.
5. **Submit the Project Deliverables.** Once the package passes the checklist, submit. Confirm as a team that the submitted version is the one everyone agreed is final.
6. **Read the final check-in page** if your team is holding the biweekly meeting. Send the presentation logistics message either way.
7. **Read What's Next** to prep for Reflection #5 and the End-of-Project Peer Evaluation.
