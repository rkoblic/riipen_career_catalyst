# Video Script: The judgment academic work never asks for

A question no week in this course has asked your team to answer yet: when is the work done?

Academic work has a simple answer. The work is done when the deadline hits. You hand in what you have.

Professional work asks the question differently. The deadline still exists, but sitting inside it is a judgment call. Is the remaining work worth more than the risk of breaking what already works? That is the question a consulting team asks before a client delivery. It is the question an editor asks before a manuscript goes to print. It is the question your team is about to make on a real deliverable.

You already have the employer's Near-Final Review feedback. Your team has one substantial production block to decide which items to act on and which to set aside. At the end of the week, your team decides the deliverable is done, not because time ran out, but because the team made the call.

That is the professional practice this week is asking you to run.
