# What's next

The deliverable is substantially done. Next week is where the package gets a final polish pass, a whole-document quality-review pass, and goes out the door.

---

## Finalize and submit

The shape of the Final Deliverables is already there. What's left next week is a final read-through, a consistency pass on formatting and language, a whole-document AI quality-review pass on the assembled package, and the submission itself. Final Deliverables carry the largest grade weight of any single deliverable in the course.

---

## Your biweekly check-in

Your standing biweekly check-in with the employer falls next week. If your team has targeted questions (whether a specific paragraph lands the way you intend, whether a particular recommendation is pitched at the right level), make use of it. Bring a short list and keep the session tight.

If your team doesn't have specific questions, it's fine to request to skip it. A short message on the platform is all it takes:

> Hi [Employer], we're in the final pass on the deliverable and don't have targeted questions we'd need the meeting to answer. If it works for you, we'd like to skip this week's check-in and regroup at the Final Presentation. Let us know either way.

Cancelling a meeting you don't need is a professional courtesy, not a lapse. The employer's time is finite.

---

## How to prepare

- Enter next week with the deliverable at near-final quality, with this week's triaged feedback addressed.
- Keep the Change Log current. Every feedback item should have the team's decision logged and, where action was taken, where the change appears in the deliverable.
- The presentation outline from this week is a working draft. Leave it where your team can find it. Rehearsal and refinement come later in the course.
- Submit Reflection #4 before the end of the week.
- Track AI use across this week's production so next week's disclosure reflects what actually happened rather than getting reconstructed.
