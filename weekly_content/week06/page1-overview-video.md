# Video Script: From planning to development

The research phase trained you to gather information before deciding. Development asks for the opposite: act before you have everything.

The biggest stall in this phase is waiting until you feel ready. You won't, not in the way research made you feel ready.

Start the draft anyway. Even badly. Starting doesn't mean writing well. It means writing headers, filling in what you're sure about even if it's three sentences, and putting "(I don't know yet)" in brackets where you're not. The draft becomes a map of what you have and where the holes are.

A draft exposes gaps that notes hide. You find out what you actually don't know, and where to go look. Most teams don't realize what's missing until they try to write it down.
