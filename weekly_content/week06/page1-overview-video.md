# Video Script: From planning to development

The kickoff, the research, the Direction Check are behind you. Your team is in development now, actually making the thing your employer is going to receive.

A consultant finishes discovery interviews and starts writing the report. A designer wraps up research and starts mocking up screens. A marketer signs off on the strategy and starts producing the campaign. The shift looks the same on every project. The feedback you ask for is no longer "is this the right direction?" It's "is this draft any good?"

Development sometimes asks you to use a skill you haven't built yet. That's normal. Find a resource. Ask a colleague. Do the research. AI can help you bridge the gap. The work has to be yours, but you don't have to start from zero.

That's development. Making the thing, learning as you go, asking for help when you need it.
