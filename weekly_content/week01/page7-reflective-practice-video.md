# Video Script: Why reflective practice matters

Think about the best professional you know. Maybe it's a manager, a mentor, someone you've worked with. What makes them good at what they do?

Chances are, it's not just that they have the right skills. It's that they've learned from experience in a way that most people don't. They can tell you exactly what they'd do differently if they could run a meeting again, or why their approach to a particular problem changed after seeing it go wrong the first time.

That's reflective practice. And it's what separates a professional who actually grows over a career from one who repeats the same year again and again.

In this course, you'll use a model called DEAL, which stands for: Describe what happened. Examine why it happened the way it did. Then articulate what you learned and how you'll use it going forward.

It sounds straightforward, and it is, but it can take some practice to get used to. Most people skip straight from "what happened" to "what I'll do next" without spending any time on the examine step in the middle.

You'll complete several DEAL reflections throughout this course, with each one building on the last. By the end, you'll have a portfolio that shows not just what you did, but what you learned from doing it. That's the kind of evidence that really helps you stand out in a professional setting.
