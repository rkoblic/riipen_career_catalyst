# How the course is run

You'll work across two platforms this term: the LMS where the course content lives, and the Riipen platform where the project work happens. This page covers what's on each, how to access them, and who to contact when something comes up.

---

## The two platforms

The split between the two systems is straightforward.

**The Learning Management System (LMS) is where the course content lives.** Everything you do for the course itself happens here, including talking to your instructor.

**The Riipen platform is where the project lives.** Everything you do for the project happens here, including communicating with your employer contact.

Grades show up in both systems. The LMS is the system of record for your overall course grade. The Riipen platform marks project-related milestones as your team hits them.

---

## What happens where

A quick reference for when you're not sure where to go.

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Course pages and videos | LMS |
| Reflections | LMS |
| Communication with your instructor | LMS |
| Project brief and project info | Riipen |
| Communication with your employer | Riipen |
| Team deliverables | Riipen |
| Employer feedback on your work | Riipen |
| Grades | Both |

## Your Riipen Experience Manager

Alongside your instructor, you'll have a Riipen Experience Manager assigned to your project. The Experience Manager is your go-to for anything related to the project itself. That includes questions about the Riipen platform and trickier moments along the way, like an employer who's gone quiet or a misunderstanding about scope.

The Experience Manager is also the primary point of contact for your employer. They onboarded the employer to the program and are available throughout the project to help the partnership stay on track.

You don't need to wait for something to go wrong to reach out. The Experience Manager is there to support the experience, not just to step in when there's a problem.

---

## Who to contact for what

When you're not sure who to reach out to:

- **Your instructor** handles anything course-related, including assignments and feedback on your individual work.
- **Your Riipen Experience Manager** handles anything project- or platform-related, including how to use Riipen and how to navigate the employer relationship.
- **Your employer contact** is your direct line on the project work itself, including the brief and any feedback they have on your team's deliverables.

When in doubt, the Experience Manager can point you in the right direction.
