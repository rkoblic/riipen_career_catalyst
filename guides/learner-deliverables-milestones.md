# Learner Deliverables — Reference for Riipen Experience Managers

Quick reference of every learner submission across the 15-week course. Use this when building milestones and due dates into the course shell.

- **Graded** = scored against a rubric and contributes to the final grade.
- **Checkpoint** = completion-based; learners pass on submission, no quality rubric.
- **Employer viewable** = whether the employer (industry partner) sees the submission. Most checkpoints and all individual deliverables are internal to the course.
- **Submission week** = the week the deliverable is due. Some deliverables are introduced or drafted earlier; that's noted in the description.

| Week | Deliverable | Individual / Team | Graded / Checkpoint | Employer viewable | Brief description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your Starting Point Questionnaire | Individual | Checkpoint | No | Self-assessment and ranked project preferences. Forms the baseline for later reflections. |
| 2 | Team Charter | Team | Checkpoint | No | Team's working agreement: roles, norms, communication channels, and decision-making. Internal team document. |
| 3 | Kickoff Summary | Team | Checkpoint | No | Internal record of what the employer said at the Kickoff. Used by the team during research; not sent to the employer. |
| 3 | Reflection #1 | Individual | Graded | No | First DEAL-format reflection. |
| 4 | Context Analysis & Project Plan | Team | Graded | Yes | First formal team deliverable. Research findings, strategic implications, and project plan. Automatically shared with the employer on submission. |
| 5 | Direction Check Summary | Team | Checkpoint | No | Internal record of what was heard at the Direction Check meeting and what the team is changing as a result. Not shared with the employer. |
| 6 | Reflection #2 | Individual | Graded | No | Second DEAL-format reflection. |
| 7 | Work-in-Progress Summary | Team | Checkpoint | No | Internal team-facing summary used to prepare for Development Check-In #1. Not sent to the employer. |
| 8 | Mid-Project Peer Evaluation | Individual | Graded | No | Peer evaluation across four Teamwork subskills using a behaviorally anchored scale. Counts toward Professional Engagement. |
| 8 | Mid-Project Employer Feedback | Team | Graded | No | Not learner-submitted — the employer fills out a brief team-level survey. Counts toward Professional Engagement. |
| 9 | Draft Deliverable | Team | Checkpoint | No | First complete internal assembly of the team's deliverable. Not sent to the employer. |
| 9 | Reflection #3 | Individual | Graded | No | Third DEAL-format reflection. Feedback-themed. |
| 11 | Near-Final Package | Team | Checkpoint | Yes | Near-final draft of the deliverable. Submitted before the Near-Final review meeting; the employer reads it in advance. |
| 12 | Reflection #4 | Individual | Graded | No | Fourth DEAL-format reflection. |
| 13 | Project Deliverables | Team | Graded | Yes | The client-facing deliverable(s) submitted to the employer. |
| 14 | Presentation Outline | Team | Checkpoint | No | Outline of the final presentation, submitted before the team builds the deck. Internal working document; not shared with the employer. |
| 15 | Final Presentation | Team | Graded | Yes | Live final presentation delivered to the employer (and any other audience). |
| 15 | End-of-Project Peer Evaluation | Individual | Graded | No | Final peer evaluation. Work begins in Week 14 and is submitted in Week 15. Counts toward Professional Engagement. |
| 15 | End-of-Project Employer Feedback | Team | Graded | No | Not learner-submitted — the employer fills out a summative team-level survey after the Final Presentation. Counts toward Professional Engagement. |
| 15 | Instructor Observation | Individual | Graded | No | Not learner-submitted — the instructor assigns a holistic 1–4 score at end of semester based on cumulative observation. Counts toward Professional Engagement. |
| 15 | Reflection #5 | Individual | Graded | No | Final DEAL-format reflection, focused on articulating learning for transfer. Work begins in Week 14 and is submitted in Week 15. |
