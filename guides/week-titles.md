# Career Catalyst — Week Titles

Quick reference for the canonical week titles used in design docs and informally aligned with Canvas modules.

| Week | Phase | Title | Focus |
|------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Foundation | Getting Started | Orientation, DEAL framework, intake |
| 2 | Foundation | Building Your Team | Team formation, charter, first employer outreach |
| 3 | Discovery | Understanding Your Client | Kickoff meeting, client research |
| 4 | Discovery | From Research to Direction | Research → strategy synthesis; first graded deliverable |
| 5 | Discovery | Sharing Your Direction | Direction Check (employer document review) |
| 6 | Discovery → Production | From Planning to Production | Phase transition, Change Log intro, Reflection #2 |
| 7 | Production | Drafting and Iterating | Peer review, Development Check-In #1 |
| 8 | Production | From Feedback to Forward Motion | Mid-Project Peer Evaluation |
| 9 | Production | Integrating Feedback | Team processing of feedback, Development Check-In #2, Reflection #3 |
| 10 | Production → Refinement | Articulating What You're Learning | Transferable learning, AI quality reviewer, integration |
| 11 | Refinement | From Building to Polishing | Near-Final Package, self-review, Near-Final Check-In |
| 12 | Refinement | From Feedback to Final | Final iteration, presentation fundamentals, Reflection #4 |
| 13 | Refinement → Integration | Finalizing Deliverables | Final Deliverables submission, optional employer Final Check-In |
| 14 | Integration | Preparing to Present | Presentation build & rehearsal, Reflection #5, Peer Evaluation #2 |
| 15 | Integration | Presenting Your Work | Final Presentation, debrief, course closure |

**Maintenance:** When a week's design-doc title changes, update this file too (it is not auto-generated). Phase labels follow the structure described in `guides/actual-content-map.md`.
