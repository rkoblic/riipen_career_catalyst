# Team Processing Agreement

*How to use this template: Complete this agreement at the end of your team processing session, after each member has shared their individual commitments from peer feedback and after the team has worked through the employer's mid-project survey together. The agreement captures both halves of that work as a single written record. It is not a formal submission. Save it to your team's shared workspace and treat it as a reference point for the rest of the project. The agreement will also become the material you draw from in Reflection #3, and the patterns named here may resurface in the second round of peer evaluation later in the course. Keep the commitments specific. A vague commitment is one nobody can follow up on.*
